# Star Squadron

A compact, original browser arcade game inspired by the fixed-shooter classics of the early 1980s.

## Play

Open `index.html` in a modern browser and select **Start Mission**.

- Move: Left/Right arrows or A/D
- Fire: Space or Z
- Pause: P
- On-screen pause/resume and exit-to-title controls are available during a mission.
- On phones and tablets, drag left or right anywhere on the game screen to steer.
- Optional auto-fire supports comfortable one-handed play and is remembered by the browser.
- The cabinet scales fluidly to different screen widths and respects iPhone safe areas.

The game stores the high score locally in your browser. Sound begins after the first button press, as required by modern browsers.

## Difficulty

- **Easy:** Four fighters, slower and less frequent enemy attacks, faster movement, and longer recovery protection.
- **Normal:** A balanced introduction to the full arcade rules.
- **Hard:** The original Star Squadron tuning from the first release.

Your selected difficulty is remembered by the browser and appears beneath the wave counter during play.

## Challenging Stages

Stages 3, 7, 11, 15, and every fourth stage thereafter are no-danger bonus rounds. Five groups of eight targets follow distinct, learnable flight paths without firing or colliding with your fighter.

- Each target: 100 points
- Each complete group: 1,000–3,000 points depending on the bonus round
- All 40 targets: 10,000-point perfect bonus

After the fifth group leaves, a results panel totals the target score, complete-group bonuses, and perfect award before normal combat resumes.

## Install on iPhone

Open the published HTTPS address in Safari, tap **Share**, choose **Add to Home Screen**, and tap **Add**. The installed game launches full-screen and works offline after its first successful load.

## Capture and rescue

Commander enemies announce a capture attempt with a slower, glowing corkscrew approach, followed by descending green radar arcs and a distinct tractor-beam sound. If you remain beneath the beam, your fighter slowly spirals and rotates upward through it before being carried into the enemy formation. Continue with a reserve fighter and destroy that commander during its next diving attack to recover your ship as a wider **Dual Fighter** with two firing lanes.

Aim carefully: the captive fighter can be hit by your own fire. A dual fighter has twice the firepower, but its wider profile is easier for enemies to hit.
